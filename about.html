Our Story

Aromas by Anu started as a small passion project — a love for the ocean, beautiful scents, and the art of candle‑making. What began in a tiny kitchen with hand‑poured wax and concrete moulds has grown into a community of candle lovers who believe in mindful living and bringing the calm of the coast into every room.

Each candle is crafted from 100% natural soy wax and housed in a hand‑finished concrete jar that doubles as décor long after the last flame burns. Every pour is unique — because nature never repeats itself, and neither do we.

Our Values

🌿 Eco‑Friendly

Natural soy wax, lead‑free wicks, and reusable concrete jars that reduce waste.

🤲 Handmade

Every candle is hand‑poured with care, making each piece truly one‑of‑a‑kind.

🌊 Ocean‑Inspired

Our scents and designs are drawn from the beauty and serenity of the sea.
